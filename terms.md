## English

These subscription terms apply to Solenya versions that offer Premium, including version 1.1. Premium provides access to meditation, breathing
exercises and nature sounds while your subscription remains active. Previous
customers of the original paid app retain access without a subscription.

Eligible new subscribers may start a one-month free trial by confirming Apple's
purchase sheet. Merely opening or installing the app does not start a trial.
Apple determines introductory offer eligibility, limited to one offer per
subscription group. After the trial, the subscription automatically renews monthly
at the local App Store price displayed before purchase. The price and billing
currency depend on your Apple Account's storefront.

Payments are handled by Apple and charged to your Apple Account. Cancel at least
24 hours before the trial or current period ends to avoid renewal. Manage or
cancel in Apple Account > Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a
subscription. Apple determines any remaining trial access after cancellation and
handles refund requests under its policies.

Restore an active subscription or original paid app access using the same Apple
Account from Solenya Premium > Restore Purchases. Progress is stored locally on
your device; subscriptions do not provide cloud backup. Internet is required for
purchases, restoration and current prices. Built-in practices can work offline
while verified access remains valid.

Apple's [standard End User License Agreement](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/)
also applies. Solenya is for general wellness, not medical diagnosis or treatment.
